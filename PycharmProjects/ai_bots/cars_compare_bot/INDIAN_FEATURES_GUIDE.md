## 🔧 Key Specifications Shown

### Engine Information
- Engine type (Petrol/Diesel/Electric)
- Engine displacement (1.2L, 1.5L, 2.0L, etc.)
- Horsepower (hp)
- Torque (Nm)

### Performance
- 0-100 km/h time
- Top speed
- Transmission type (Manual/Automatic/CVT)

### Efficiency
- Fuel consumption (km/l for petrol/diesel)
- Electric efficiency (km/kWh for Tesla)
- Estimated range on full tank

### Comfort & Space
- Number of seats (5 or 7)
- Cargo capacity (liters)
- Transmission options

## 📱 How to Use

### Step 1: Set Your Preferences
- Open the sidebar (left side)
- Choose comparison type (specs, price, features, etc.)
- Select vehicle category (SUV, Sedan, Electric, etc.)
- Set price range using the slider (in ₹ Lakhs)
- Select your top priorities (Performance, Fuel Efficiency, etc.)

### Step 2: Ask Your Question
- Type your car comparison question
- Examples:
  - "Compare Hyundai Creta with Tata Nexon"
  - "Best SUV under ₹10 lakhs"
  - "Show fuel efficiency comparison"
- Click "Send" button

### Step 3: Get Detailed Results
- Specifications table with all key data
- Feature comparisons
- Safety ratings
- Pros and cons
- AI-powered recommendation

## ⚙️ Technical Details

- **Currency**: All prices in Indian Rupees (₹)
- **Measurement**: 
  - 1 Lakh = ₹100,000
  - 10 Lakhs = ₹10,00,000
- **Efficiency Units**:
  - Petrol/Diesel: km/l (kilometers per liter)
  - Electric: km/kWh (kilometers per kilowatt-hour)
- **Speed**: km/h (kilometers per hour)

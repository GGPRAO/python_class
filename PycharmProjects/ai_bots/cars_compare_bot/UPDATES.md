## How to Use the Updated Bot

1. **Select comparison preferences** in the left sidebar
2. **Choose price range** using the slider (in ₹ Lakhs)
3. **Pick priority factors** (Performance, Fuel Economy, Safety, etc.)
4. **Ask a question** about cars, such as:
   - "Compare Hyundai Creta vs Tata Nexon"
   - "What's the best SUV under ₹10 lakhs?"
   - "Show me electric cars in India"
   - "Compare fuel efficiency of compact SUVs"

5. **View results** in a formatted table with:
   - Detailed specifications
   - Feature comparisons
   - Safety ratings
   - Pros and cons
   - AI recommendations

🔄 WHAT IS SYNC?

When you change Payment → Maintenance auto-updates (for current month only)
When you change Maintenance → Payment auto-updates (for current month only)
This keeps Payment and Maintenance CONSISTENT for the current month.

❓ FAQ:

Q: Why is sync only for current month?
A: To ensure consistency between Payment and Maintenance for the current month,
   while allowing flexibility for past/future months.

Q: Can I have maintenance for 2026-03 but payment NOT PAID?
A: Yes! Only current month (2026-02) syncs. You can have any maintenance
   months independent of payment status.

Q: What happens when current month changes (March 2026)?
A: The sync will work with the new current month. Old synced pairs remain
   in the database as historical data.

Q: How do I see sync in action?
A: 1. Open http://127.0.0.1:5000/
   2. Change a Payment dropdown
   3. Watch the Maintenance column update automatically
   4. See flash message explain the sync
